# 🌌 Akashic Records
### **AI-Powered Skill Intelligence & Competency Platform for India's Official Statistical System**
> **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) — Problem Statement 26101**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary & Problem Statement Mapping

India’s Official Statistical System (led by the **Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation - MoSPI**) relies on precise data collection, high-frequency indicator compilation, and national accounting. However, evolving data ecosystems demand modern proficiencies across **multi-stage survey sampling, automated price index calculations, and big data pipelines**.

**Akashic Records** is an AI-driven skill intelligence platform built to:
1. **Identify Competency Gaps:** Evaluate statistical personnel and students against official benchmarks (NSSO, PLFS, CPI, IIP, and National Accounts SNA 2008).
2. **Recommend iGOT Karmayogi Pathways:** Generate personalized learning roadmaps with micro-credentials and practical data labs.
3. **Provide an AI Statistical Copilot:** An intelligent assistant trained on MoSPI survey protocols, index weighting formulas, and data confidentiality standards.
4. **Adaptive Assessment Engine:** Real-time MCQ evaluations with instant methodological rationales that dynamically boost competency scores on the live dashboard.
